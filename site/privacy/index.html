Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 21, 2026

1. Overview

uttero is a macOS app that runs on your Mac. It has no servers and no accounts. This policy explains what the app does with your data and which third parties it talks to on your behalf.

2. What stays on your Mac

  • Wake phrase listening: if you turn on the wake phrase, audio is recognized on your Mac by Apple's on-device speech recognizer. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Settings and your OpenAI API key: settings are stored in macOS preferences; the key is stored in your Keychain.
  • Manager state and memory: the manager's session reference, recent conversation lines and its notes are stored in ~/.uttero-osx/ as plain files you can read, edit or delete.
  • Logs: the app writes a log to ~/Library/Logs/uttero/. It is not uploaded.

3. Third parties

uttero sends data to these services only to do what you ask:

  • OpenAI: during a voice call, your microphone audio and the conversation are streamed to OpenAI's GPT-Live API using your own API key. OpenAI processes it under its privacy policy and your API agreement with them.
  • Anthropic, through Claude Code: your requests are handled by Claude Code on your Mac, signed in with your own account. Claude Code sends them to Anthropic under Anthropic's privacy policy and the terms of your account. If the manager uses your MCP servers or connectors, those services receive what the request needs.
  • GitHub: to check for updates, the app downloads the update feed and update files from github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com. GitHub may log these requests as with any download.

4. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and we don't receive your audio, transcripts, code or keys.

5. This website

uttero.dev is a static site hosted on Netlify. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Netlify may keep standard server logs. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and a script from jsDelivr, and read the update feed from GitHub to show the latest version.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: for calls and the wake phrase.
  • Speech Recognition: for the on-device wake phrase.
  • Notifications: to show approvals and updates when no call is open.

You can revoke any of these in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

7. Children

uttero is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
